# sbb 2.0.0, benchmark 1.0.0 (draft)

Draft notes for the GitHub release. The tag mints the Zenodo DOI.

## Benchmark 1.0

- `benchmarks/v1.0.yaml`: the 0.9 scenarios and tracks (N-MNIST lite, DVS128 Gesture and
  CIFAR10-DVS full, three seeds each), and the full matrix of 14 attacks against 15 defenses,
  1845 cells, 45 of them not applicable.
- New attacks: T-Backdoor (rate, latency, jitter), replicated. TaCT, Adap-Blend and Adap-Patch,
  ANN image attacks ported to SNNs and event data, not attacks designed for SNNs, so they have
  no replication record.
- New defenses: NDSBM, STRIP per time step, and two SNN-native baselines, fewer time steps and
  threshold recalibration.
- `sneaky_dynamic` declares `keeps_clean_accuracy: false`, so its cells skip the validity check
  and report both clean accuracies. Every one of its full-track seeds of 0.9.0 failed the check
  (drops of 3.5 to 11.8 points on DVS128 Gesture, 20 to 29 on CIFAR10-DVS), as its joint training
  costs clean accuracy in the paper too. It stays at 1.2.0, and its 48 `invalid_attack` results of
  0.9.0 reran from the defense step on.
- Changed versions: activation clustering 1.3.0 and spectral signatures 1.1.0 (they reuse the
  victim as their reference model, which changes their GPU numbers), NDSBM 1.1.0.
- Results: all 1845 cells. 621 are carried over unchanged from 0.9.0 and 1224 ran on the
  maintainers' cluster in 160 GPU hours, on NVIDIA GH200. 1695 cells ended ok, 105
  invalid_attack (the attack cost more clean accuracy than the budget allows, rule 6) and 45
  not_applicable.

## Findings

Mean ASR over the effective oblivious attacks, in percent, with the clean accuracy after the
defense. Each attack counts once, as the mean of its cell over the valid seeds, as on the
leaderboard. A defense costs clean accuracy against an attack when its mean CA falls more than 3
points below the undefended one.

- Four attacks never worked on DVS128 Gesture: static patch (4.2, one valid seed), T-Backdoor
  latency (4.9) and jitter (3.0), and Adap-Patch (44.3), against a natural ASR of 1.5. They are
  left out of these means, the Pareto view and the model AUROC.
- Undefended, the attacks reach an ASR of 99.6 on N-MNIST (lite), 97.0 on DVS128 Gesture and
  99.6 on CIFAR10-DVS.
- No defense works everywhere. STRIP leads on N-MNIST (39.5, CA 97.5) and activation clustering
  on CIFAR10-DVS (25.8, CA 67.0), and neither costs clean accuracy against any attack. On DVS128
  Gesture no defense lowers the ASR much without that cost: spectral signatures reaches 56.3
  over the 5 attacks it applies to and costs clean accuracy against one, and fine-pruning 61.4,
  costing it against 4 of 7. STRIP is the strongest that never costs it (78.0, CA 90.2).
- NDSBM lowers the N-MNIST ASR to 47.2 but its clean accuracy falls to 29.8 on the tiny lite
  network, as noted for NDSBM 1.1.0.
- The SNN-native baselines are weak. Fewer time steps leaves the ASR nearly unchanged.
  Threshold recalibration lowers it to 89.4 on N-MNIST and 76.5 on DVS128 Gesture, where it
  costs clean accuracy against 4 of 7 attacks. On CIFAR10-DVS it leaves the model unchanged in
  13 of its 14 cells.
- The adaptive attacks beat the defenses they target only in part, and some results split
  between seeds. TaCT keeps its ASR against both STRIPs on every seed on DVS128 Gesture and
  N-MNIST (100 and 99.7) and loses some on CIFAR10-DVS (81.0 against STRIP with seeds from 68 to
  97, 76.0 per time step with seeds from 55 to 87). Adap-Blend keeps its backdoor against
  activation clustering on both valid DVS128 Gesture seeds (99.6 and 64.4), on 2 of 3
  CIFAR10-DVS seeds (0, 100 and 98.2) and on no N-MNIST seed. Activation clustering removes
  Adap-Patch on N-MNIST (12.4, seeds from 1.8 to 25.7), not on CIFAR10-DVS (69.3).

## Verification

- `VerificationPolicy` computes every status from the results and the records. A full-track
  cell is `reproduced` when an independent group matches a spot check of its manifest.
- `sbb spot-check MANIFEST` picks 3 full-track results per manifest, seeded by its bundle id.
- `docs/verifying.md` walks a verifier through setup, the model zoo, spot checks and the pull
  request. TODO([FILL]): the independent group and its spot checks.
- Count metrics (`synops`) are judged relative to their value.
- A verification record holds facts only: the result's numbers it checked, the rerun's outcome
  and numbers. Verdicts are judged when read, so the site's verification log and the statuses
  always agree. A record of numbers the result no longer has shows as `outdated`. The 420 records
  of 0.9.0 and 1.0.0 were rewritten in this form, each covering its result, and no status changed.
- verify-lite reruns every lite result that no record covers (`sbb unverified`), not only
  results without any record file.
- The lite tolerance `lite_pp` is 10.0 (3.0 in benchmark 0.9). Lite results come from GPUs
  and verify-lite reruns them on GitHub CPUs, where discrete choices of some defenses flip.

## Adaptive track

- An attack card may declare `knowledge.defense: aware:<defense>[,<defense>...]`. Aware attacks
  run against every defense like any other attack, and the site groups them as adaptive.
- A source-specific attack (TaCT) declares `source_classes`, and its success metrics count only
  those classes.

## Metrics and leaderboard

- Every cell run for 1.0 reports `spike_rate`, `spike_rate_triggered` and `synops`. The 621
  cells carried over from 0.9.0 lack them, since sbb 0.1 did not measure activity.
- The leaderboard flags three things, each judged against the same attack without a defense:
  an attack whose mean ASR stays below 50% (`ineffective_attack`), a defense whose mean CA falls
  more than the budget's 3 points below (`costly_defense`, the defense validity rule), and a
  defense that left CA, ASR and RA as they were on every seed (`unchanged`). Ineffective attacks
  leave the Pareto means, the broken-by lists and the model AUROC. A defense that costs clean
  accuracy against an attack of a group cannot be on that group's Pareto front.
- Tables and CSVs show the 95% t-interval over seeds.
- New Pareto view (`#pareto`): clean accuracy against 100 minus ASR per defense and attack group.
- Defense pages list the adaptive attacks that break them (mean ASR of 50% or more).
- The table shows where each cell ran (sites and devices).
- Every text of the page names a method or dataset the same way (STRIP, Sneaky Smart,
  N-MNIST), from the card or the dataset class. CSV downloads keep the ids.

## Fixes from the v2 code review

None changes a number: 20 synthetic cells give byte-identical results before and after.

- `sbb reproduce` judges full-track reruns with the seed-based tolerance of the
  VerificationPolicy, so the stored verdict and the site agree.
- A TaCT source or cover class without samples fails the cell before any training, instead of
  poisoning silently without cover samples.
- The triggered test pass skips the synaptic operations, which no metric reads.
- The Pareto view shows defenses without results against every attack of a group apart.
- Results from the container image record the commit it was built from and its `uv.lock` hash,
  both `unknown` before. Slurm tasks in an image pinned by digest record the digest.
- Known, for Adap-Blend 1.1.0 with the next benchmark version: its pattern scales with the
  sample's peak count, so a test sample with no events gets no trigger.

## Fixes from the external review of 2026-11-25

None changes a result: every cell of the site's `data.json` is the same apart from its flags.

- The leaderboard flags ineffective attacks, defenses that cost clean accuracy and defenses that
  left the model unchanged (Metrics and leaderboard). These findings come from the flagged data.
- The findings above count each attack once, as its cell mean over valid seeds. They pooled the
  seed results before, which weighed attacks by their number of valid seeds.
- Docs: the lite track's results ran on a GPU, the defenders' clean subset is a share of the
  train split, the attack validity rule names the `keeps_clean_accuracy` exemption, and the
  DVS128 Gesture test set has 288 samples (264 of them outside the target class).
- Known, for the next benchmark version, since each moves numbers: an attack validity reference
  that does not hinge on one clean model, a static patch sized per dataset, Adap-Blend and
  Adap-Patch with the papers' payload, TaCT with the paper's payload and a metric for flips
  outside the source class, NDSBM on networks without a spiking output layer, a defender pool
  held out of the victim's training, and the pool sizes of STRIP and ANP on DVS128 Gesture.

## Changes for plugin authors

- Specs, site profiles, cards and records reject unknown keys, so a typo fails when the file
  loads. Options sbb cannot run fail there too: runtime `docker`, scheduler `hfjobs`,
  `apptainer` without Slurm or an image, partial data knowledge, `amp`, the federated kind.
- A plugin module missing from its package `__init__` fails the contract tests.
- Every card states a `display_name`, the name the leaderboard shows, unique per role. Every
  dataset class states one too. Card ids, versions and cell keys do not change.
- Every card may state an `adaptation_note` (it moved from the defense card to the base card).
- `SNNModel.steps(x)` returns the output at every time step, `[T, B, K]`.
- `ModelDefense.extra` stores state outside the weights next to the checkpoint.
- Artifact keys hash an artifact epoch (`sbb.ARTIFACT_EPOCH`, "1.0") instead of the package
  major.minor, so models cached by sbb 1.0 stay valid in 2.0. The epoch moves only when a
  change alters what an artifact holds.
- `sbb carry-over SPEC --source VERSION` copies the results of an earlier benchmark version
  whose cells did not change, with their verify-lite records. Benchmark 1.0 carries 621
  results of 0.9.0. Each names its original in `Provenance.carried_from`.
