# sbb 2.0.0, benchmark 1.0.0 (draft)

Draft notes for the GitHub release. The tag mints the Zenodo DOI.

## Benchmark 1.0

- `benchmarks/v1.0.yaml`: the 0.9 scenarios and tracks (N-MNIST lite, DVS128 Gesture and
  CIFAR10-DVS full, three seeds each), and the full matrix of 14 attacks against 15 defenses,
  1845 cells, 45 of them not applicable.
- New attacks: T-Backdoor (rate, latency, jitter), replicated. TaCT, Adap-Blend and Adap-Patch,
  ANN image attacks ported to SNNs and event data, not attacks designed for SNNs, so they have
  no replication record.
- New defenses: NDSBM, STRIP per time step, and two SNN-native baselines, fewer time steps and
  threshold recalibration.
- Changed versions: activation clustering 1.3.0 and spectral signatures 1.1.0 (they reuse the
  victim as their reference model, which changes their GPU numbers), NDSBM 1.1.0.
- Results: all 1845 cells. 621 are carried over unchanged from 0.9.0 and 1224 ran on the
  maintainers' cluster in 160 GPU hours, on NVIDIA GH200. 1695 cells ended ok, 105
  invalid_attack (the attack cost more clean accuracy than the budget allows, rule 6) and 45
  not_applicable.

## Findings

Mean ASR over the oblivious attacks, in percent, with the clean accuracy after the defense.

- Undefended, the attacks reach an ASR of 99.6 on N-MNIST (lite), 75.5 on DVS128 Gesture and
  99.6 on CIFAR10-DVS.
- No defense works everywhere. The strongest one per scenario is STRIP on N-MNIST (39.5, CA
  97.5), spectral signatures on DVS128 Gesture (43.1, CA 90.4) and activation clustering on
  CIFAR10-DVS (24.2, CA 67.1).
- NDSBM lowers the N-MNIST ASR to 47.2 but its clean accuracy falls to 29.9 on the tiny lite
  network, as noted for NDSBM 1.1.0.
- The SNN-native baselines are weak. Fewer time steps leaves the ASR nearly unchanged.
  Threshold recalibration only helps on DVS128 Gesture (59.3).
- The adaptive attacks beat the defenses they target only in part. TaCT keeps its ASR against
  both STRIPs on DVS128 Gesture and N-MNIST (100 and 99.7) and loses some on CIFAR10-DVS (81.0
  against STRIP, 76.0 per time step). Adap-Blend beats activation clustering on DVS128 Gesture
  (82.0) and in part on CIFAR10-DVS (66.1), not on N-MNIST (0.0). Activation clustering still
  catches Adap-Patch on DVS128 Gesture (11.0) and N-MNIST (12.4), not on CIFAR10-DVS (69.3).

## Verification

- `VerificationPolicy` computes every status from the results and the records. A full-track
  cell is `reproduced` when an independent group matches a spot check of its manifest.
- `sbb spot-check MANIFEST` picks 3 full-track results per manifest, seeded by its bundle id.
- `docs/verifying.md` walks a verifier through setup, the model zoo, spot checks and the pull
  request. TODO([FILL]): the independent group and its spot checks.
- Count metrics (`synops`) are judged relative to their value.

## Adaptive track

- An attack card may declare `knowledge.defense: aware:<defense>[,<defense>...]`. Aware attacks
  run against every defense like any other attack, and the site groups them as adaptive.
- A source-specific attack (TaCT) declares `source_classes`, and its success metrics count only
  those classes.

## Metrics and leaderboard

- Every cell reports `spike_rate`, `spike_rate_triggered` and `synops`.
- Tables and CSVs show the 95% t-interval over seeds.
- New Pareto view (`#pareto`): clean accuracy against 100 minus ASR per defense and attack group.
- Defense pages list the adaptive attacks that break them (mean ASR of 50% or more).
- The table shows where each cell ran (sites and devices).

## Fixes from the v2 code review

None changes a number: 20 synthetic cells give byte-identical results before and after.

- `sbb reproduce` judges full-track reruns with the seed-based tolerance of the
  VerificationPolicy, so the stored verdict and the site agree.
- A TaCT source or cover class without samples fails the cell before any training, instead of
  poisoning silently without cover samples.
- The triggered test pass skips the synaptic operations, which no metric reads.
- The Pareto view shows defenses without results against every attack of a group apart.
- Known, for Adap-Blend 1.1.0 with the next benchmark version: its pattern scales with the
  sample's peak count, so a test sample with no events gets no trigger.

## Changes for plugin authors

- Every card may state an `adaptation_note` (it moved from the defense card to the base card).
- `SNNModel.steps(x)` returns the output at every time step, `[T, B, K]`.
- `ModelDefense.extra` stores state outside the weights next to the checkpoint.
- Artifact keys hash an artifact epoch (`sbb.ARTIFACT_EPOCH`, "1.0") instead of the package
  major.minor, so models cached by sbb 1.0 stay valid in 2.0. The epoch moves only when a
  change alters what an artifact holds.
- `sbb carry-over SPEC --source VERSION` copies the results of an earlier benchmark version
  whose cells did not change, with their verify-lite records. Benchmark 1.0 carries 621
  results of 0.9.0. Each names its original in `Provenance.carried_from`.
